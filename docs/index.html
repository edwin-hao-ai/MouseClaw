How to actually use it

具体怎么用

🦞 Summon the AI召唤 AI

  1. Press and hold ⌘ ⇧ Space (you can change it during setup)
  2. Speak naturally — "summarize this page in three sentences", "draft a reply to my boss about taking the weekend off", "explain this error message"
  3. Let go. Your speech is recognized locally, your screen is captured, and the AI streams its reply into a speech bubble next to the pet
  4. If the AI suggests a piece of text, you can tell it to type that into your current cursor position — no copy-paste needed
  5. 按住 ⌘ ⇧ 空格（首次启动时可以改）
  6. 说话就行 —— "三句话总结这个页面"、"帮我婉拒老板加班"、"这个报错什么意思"
  7. 松开。你说的话在本地识别完，AI 看到你屏幕上的内容，回复就流到桌宠头顶的气泡里
  8. 如果你觉得 AI 的回答可以直接用，让它帮你打到光标位置 —— 不用复制粘贴

💡 Tip: hold the shortcut + drag left mouse to draw a circle on screen. The AI sees your highlight baked into the screenshot. 💡 小技巧：按住快捷键时按左键拖动 → 屏幕上画粉红圈圈 → AI 看到的截图带你的高亮。圈个报错让它解释，圈个网页区域让它总结。

🎙 Type with your voice用嘴打字

  1. Click into any input box (a Slack message, a search bar, a code editor, an email reply)
  2. Press and hold fn (the function key, bottom-left of most Mac keyboards) — you can switch this to ⌥ / ⌃ / right-shift if you don't have fn
  3. Speak. Characters appear in the input box in real-time as you talk. Punctuation gets added automatically
  4. Let go of fn. The text stays. Use it like any other typed text — backspace, copy, send
  5. 把光标放到任何输入框里（微信、搜索、邮件、代码编辑器都行）
  6. 按住 fn 键（Mac 键盘左下角那个）—— 没 fn 键可以改成 ⌥/⌃/右 shift
  7. 说话。字一边说一边出现在光标位置。中英文混合可以、标点自动加
  8. 松开 fn。字就留在那。和你手打的字一样，可以删、可以发

💡 Tip: works in Chinese, English, or mixed. Try saying "今晚 6 点半 dinner together?" — both languages come out right. 💡 小技巧：中英文混合识别。试试说"今晚 6 点半 dinner 吗"—— 中英文都出来对的。不用切输入法。

🍽 Feed it a file喂它一份文件

  1. Find a file in Finder — a PDF, a screenshot, a web page saved as HTML, a code file, even a Word doc
  2. Drag it onto the pet (the pet's mouth opens to "eat" it). You'll see "🍽 Eaten: filename.pdf" appear above the pet
  3. Now talk to the pet — "summarize in three sentences", "what's the conclusion?", "is there anything I missed?". The AI reads the file and answers
  4. 在 Finder 找一份文件 —— PDF / 截图 / 网页 / 代码 / Word 文档都行
  5. 拖到桌宠身上（老鼠会张嘴吞下）。桌宠头顶气泡显示 "🍽 已吃下：文件名.pdf"
  6. 然后就跟它说话 —— "三句话总结"、"重点是什么"、"我漏看了什么"。AI 读完文件再回你

💡 Tip: drop multiple files at once. The pet eats them all and answers based on everything. 💡 小技巧：一次拖多个文件也行，老鼠全吃下，AI 综合所有文件回答你。

📜 Continue a past chat接着上次的话题聊

  1. Click the menu bar mouse icon → "View history". Or click the pet → "History"
  2. Browse past conversations — they're searchable by what you asked
  3. Found the one you want to revive? Click "💬 Continue this topic". A panel opens with the conversation context already loaded
  4. Type or speak your follow-up. The AI remembers everything from before
  5. 点菜单栏的老鼠图标 → "查看历史记录"。也可以点桌宠 → "历史记录"
  6. 翻找以前的对话 —— 都记着，可以搜索
  7. 找到想接着聊的那条？点 "💬 继续这个话题"。会开一个新窗口，AI 已经知道你们之前聊了啥
  8. 用嘴或者打字接着问。它记得所有上下文，不用重复说

🦞 Pet follows your cursor桌宠跟着光标走

By default the pet sleeps in the corner. But during AI summon (only AI summon — not voice typing), the pet follows your mouse around. You see it land on whatever you're pointing at while you talk. It looks alive. Toggle in the tray menu. 默认情况下老鼠在屏幕角落睡觉。但当你召唤 AI 时（只有 AI 召唤、不是语音输入），老鼠会跟着你鼠标走 —— 你指哪它就过去哪。看起来像活的。托盘菜单里可以开关。
